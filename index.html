About Vivi

Vivi has been doing nails for six years, with a focus on freehand art rather than pre-made stickers or wraps — every set is painted at the chair. The studio is small on purpose: one client at a time, no rushing.

Based in Los Angeles. Walk-ins are welcome when there's an open slot, but booking ahead is the best way to get the appointment time you want.
